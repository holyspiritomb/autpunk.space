the worst thing about the transmedicalist paradigm of transness, which is a pathology paradigm, is how its proponents will just straight-up deny that it’s based on a particular value system. they will claim it is the Objective Truth backed up Science that transness is Deviation from Normal, and that Normal is even a scientifically verifiable thing rather than a widely held opinion.

they will present science itself as Objective Facts Unaffected by Human Bias, and that people who disagree with transmedicalism are Just Factually Wrong… rather than, having different values, like, for instance, “medical authority should not determine what sociopolitical groups i am permitted to belong to” and “transition and detransition are acts of reclaiming one’s body from medical authority”

transmedicalism is what you get when one’s education about transness is not sufficiently liberatory. you get trans people who desire to become oppressors within the bounds of a system that does its hardest to push them down, and they will not believe that they share a struggle with people who are outside their criteria of transness. while they simultaneously benefit from decades of trans-liberation activism by the very people they do not consider to be trans.
